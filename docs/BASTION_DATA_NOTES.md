# Bastion Data Notes — v0.4

Source-backed features:
- Recipe pages provide Research trivial, ingredients and containers.
- Item pages provide recipe usage relationships.
- NPC/zone pages provide drop tables for research materials.
- v0.4 stores concise source hints for selected components rather than pretending to contain an exhaustive drop database.

Examples verified during this build:
- Words of Expertise is used in numerous Bastion Research recipes.
- imp vanquisher in The Caverns of Exile can drop Words of Expertise.
- Shai`Din overseers in Chardok include Words of Expertise in their research drop table.
- mountain/devastating pookas include many GoD-era research words/runes such as Rune of Gale, Rune of Tempest, Words of Expertise and Words of Recourse.
